What is real, and what is recorded.

Each recording is the sequence of answers the real server gave while the real drill engine played one scenario against the simulated plant, one frame every five simulated seconds, for twelve simulated minutes.

It is one run, and its amber and red times are the same as the five-run medians on the Technology page. Other runs can differ by a few seconds. The plant and the Witness Nodes are simulated.

Under the trust light, the demo adds one view that the real software does not have: the same moment four ways. What the plant's screen claims, what the witnesses report, what HydroWitness concludes, and what the simulation says was really happening. The first three are exactly what the real software saw and said. The fourth exists only in a simulation. A real plant gives no such column, and HydroWitness never sees it. We compare it with a plain alarm panel on the Technology page.

What needs the downloadable version.

Breaking things yourself and typing in your own readings change the plant, and a recording can't answer that. In the downloadable version you can do both: it runs on your own computer with one double-click, and it never leaves your machine.
