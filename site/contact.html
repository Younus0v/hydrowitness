What helps most.

Operators and managers

Tell us how your system is monitored today, who is on call, and what you would do if the screen froze at 2 a.m.

Water-sector and security people

Tell us where the idea is weak. A careful forgery only turns red with a person's reading, and we would like to hear how you would close that.

Mentors and judges

Ask for the downloadable version, the saved test results, or a walk through the drills.

Please don't send passwords, access details or anything that identifies a real plant's security setup. We don't need them, and HydroWitness never asks for write access to anything.
