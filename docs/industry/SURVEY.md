# Eight-question survey for water operators

Send as a form or a plain email. It must take **under five minutes**. Do not ask for a name, a system name, a location, a phone number or any security detail. State at the top: "Anonymous. Student competition project. Please don't include anything about your system's security."

1. Which best describes you? (operator / superintendent or manager / rural water association staff / engineer or consultant / other)
2. About how many people does your system serve? (under 500 / 500 to 3,300 / 3,301 to 10,000 / over 10,000)
3. How many storage tanks and pumps does your largest site have? (open numbers)
4. If your SCADA or main screen froze or went blank, how would you find out the real tank level? (tick all that apply: walk to the tank / call someone on site / a local gauge or sight glass / backup radio or panel / we don't have a way / other)
5. How long would it take someone to physically reach your most important tank or pump at 2 a.m.? (under 15 min / 15 to 30 / 30 to 60 / over an hour)
6. In the last 12 months, how many times did SCADA or a remote screen show data you didn't trust? (0 / 1 to 2 / 3 to 5 / more than 5 / I don't know)
7. How many false alarms from a new monitoring tool per month would make you stop looking at it? (0 / 1 / 2 to 3 / 4 to 10 / more)
8. What is the one thing that would stop you from trying a read-only monitor that doesn't touch your control system? (free text)

At the end: "Would you be willing to talk for 20 minutes? If so, leave an email here. It will be kept separately from your answers." (An optional separate field.)

## How to use the results

* Count answers. Report as "N of M respondents" and say who was asked and how. Never write "most operators" from a few dozen answers collected from friends.
* Say how people were reached (an association mailing list, a LinkedIn group, personal contacts), because that decides who answered.
* Keep the raw answers in a private file. Publish only totals.
