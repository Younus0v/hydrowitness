# Industry kit: how to get real feedback from the people who run small water systems

**Status: drafts for the team to use. Nothing in this folder reports a result.** No interview, survey, pilot or letter of support exists yet. When one does, record it in `OUTREACH_LOG.csv` (names stay private unless the person agrees to be named) and only then change a claim on the website.

| file | use it to |
|---|---|
| `INTERVIEW_GUIDE.md` | Run a 20-minute call with a water operator and ask the right questions without selling |
| `SURVEY.md` | Send the same eight questions to many operators (a form or an email reply) |
| `PILOT_PROPOSAL.md` | Offer a small utility a read-only, no-cost "shadow mode" trial |
| `OUTREACH.md` | Emails to operators, rural water associations, university and professional reviewers, and a letter-of-support request |
| `OUTREACH_LOG.csv` | An empty log. One row per contact, so we never claim more than we have |

## Rules the whole team follows

1. **Learn, don't pitch.** The goal of an interview is to find out how things work and where we are wrong. A "that would never work" is the most useful answer we can get.
2. **Never claim what we do not have.** No "operators told us" until they did. No "pilot" until a utility agreed in writing. No "tested on a pump" figure until a recorded test exists (`docs/PUMP_TEST_PROTOCOL.md`).
3. **Protect people.** An adult (teacher, parent, mentor) knows about every contact with an outside person and is copied on first emails. We collect a role and the rough size of the system, not names, addresses, phone numbers or anything about a system's security setup. We never ask an operator to send us passwords, network diagrams or SCADA screenshots.
4. **Never touch a real plant.** No access to anyone's control system. HydroWitness is read-only by design, and a first pilot uses data the utility chooses to give us.
5. **Say who we are.** Students in a competition, a prototype, not a certified safety system.
6. **Write down what they said, in their words, the same day.** Then decide what to change.
